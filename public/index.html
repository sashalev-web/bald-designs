The idea

A bald head can be a canvas.

BaldDesigns are thin, sticker-like plastic sheets made to sit smoothly on skin. Choose a look, press it on, and treat your scalp like the expressive surface it is.

Hair was always a statement. This is the option for when you don’t have any — and still want to say something.
